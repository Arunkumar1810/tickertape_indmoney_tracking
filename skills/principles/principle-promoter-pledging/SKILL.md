# principle-promoter-pledging

Promoter pledging risk and related-party transaction siphoning evaluation engine.

## Core Thresholds

### 1. Promoter Pledged Shares (%)
- $\le 10.0\%$: Safe / Minimal margin call risk
- $10.1\% - 25.0\%$: Moderate warning threshold
- $25.1\% - 50.0\%$: Severe risk zone (vulnerable to sharp market sell-offs)
- $> 50.0\%$: **Critical Red Flag / High Insolvency Vulnerability (Hard Veto)**

### 2. Related-Party Transactions (RPT)
- $\le 5.0\%$ of revenue: Normal operating arrangements
- $5.1\% - 10.0\%$: Moderate warning threshold
- $10.1\% - 25.0\%$: Elevated conflict-of-interest risk
- $> 25.0\%$: **Severe Siphoning Risk / Unacceptable Self-Dealing (Hard Veto)**

# principle-net-net-working-capital

Evaluates deep-value liquidation cushions:
- Net Current Asset Value (NCAV): Current Assets - Total Liabilities
- Net-Net Working Capital (NNWC): Cash + 0.75*Receivables + 0.5*Inventory - Total Liabilities

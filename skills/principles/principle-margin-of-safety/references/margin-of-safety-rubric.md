# Margin of Safety & Valuation Yield Rubric

Warren Buffett's cardinal rule of investing: Never overpay. A business can be wonderful, but paying too high a multiple destroys future returns.

## Formula

$$\text{Owner Earnings Yield} = \frac{\text{True Owner Earnings}}{\text{Market Capitalization}} \times 100$$

## Hurdle Rates & Classification

| Owner Earnings Yield | Classification | Status | Principle Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| $\ge 6.0\%$ | Wide Margin of Safety | PASS | 100 |
| $4.0\% \le \text{Yield} < 6.0\%$ | Fair Value / Adequate Margin | PASS | 65 |
| $2.0\% \le \text{Yield} < 4.0\%$ | Narrow Margin of Safety | WARN | 35 |
| $< 2.0\%$ or Negative | Stretched Valuation Multiple | FAIL | 0 |

Broad-market index ETFs (e.g. VOO, SPY, QQQ) automatically pass with a score of 100 due to natural basket diversification.

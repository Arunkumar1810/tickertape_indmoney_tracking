# Economic Moat & Capital Efficiency (ROIC) Rubric

Warren Buffett's first pillar of compounding: A business must possess a durable economic moat that protects high returns on capital from competitors.

## Formula

$$\text{ROIC} = \frac{\text{NOPAT}}{\text{Invested Capital}} = \frac{\text{EBIT} \times (1 - \text{Effective Tax Rate})}{\text{Total Equity} + \text{Total Debt} - \text{Cash \& Equivalents}}$$

## Hurdle Rates & Classification

| ROIC Range | Moat Classification | Status | Principle Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| $\ge 20.0\%$ | Wide Economic Moat | PASS | 100 |
| $15.0\% \le \text{ROIC} < 20.0\%$ | Strong Economic Moat | PASS | 80 |
| $10.0\% \le \text{ROIC} < 15.0\%$ | Moderate Moat (Meets Cost of Capital) | WARN | 50 |
| $5.0\% \le \text{ROIC} < 10.0\%$ | Narrow Moat | WARN | 25 |
| $< 5.0\%$ or Negative | No Moat / Capital Destructive | FAIL | 0 |

Broad-market index ETFs (e.g. VOO, SPY, QQQ) automatically pass with a score of 100 due to natural basket diversification.

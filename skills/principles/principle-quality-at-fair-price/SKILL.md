# principle-quality-at-fair-price

Charlie Munger Quality Business at a Fair Price Evaluation Engine.

> *“Forget what you know about buying fair businesses at wonderful prices; instead, buy wonderful businesses at fair prices.”* — Charlie Munger

## Overview
Evaluates the core thesis that revolutionized Berkshire Hathaway:
1. **Business Quality Gate**: ROIC $\ge 15.0\%$ (stellar $\ge 20.0\%$) and healthy operating margins ($\ge 15.0\%$).
2. **Rejection of Value Traps**: A statistically low P/E or low P/B on a business that earns $< 10\%$ ROIC is classified as an **Uninvestable Value Trap / Cigar Butt** and failed.
3. **Reasonable Valuation Multiples**: For a wonderful business (ROIC $> 20\%$), Munger is comfortable paying up to a reasonable multiple (P/E $20 - 30\times$), while demanding an Owner Earnings Yield $\ge 4.0\%$.

## CLI Usage

```bash
node skills/principles/principle-quality-at-fair-price/scripts/evaluate_quality.js --ticker INFY
```

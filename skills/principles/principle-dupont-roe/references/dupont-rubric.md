# 5-Step DuPont ROE Quality & Financial Leverage Rubric

Charlie Munger emphasized looking under the hood of high Return on Equity (ROE) to distinguish between genuine operational excellence and risky financial leverage.

## Formula

$$\text{ROE} = \text{Tax Burden} \times \text{Interest Burden} \times \text{Operating Margin} \times \text{Asset Turnover} \times \text{Financial Leverage}$$

Where:
- $\text{Tax Burden} = \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{EBT}}$
- $\text{Interest Burden} = \frac{\text{EBT}}{\text{EBIT}}$
- $\text{Operating Margin} = \frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Revenue}}$
- $\text{Asset Turnover} = \frac{\text{Revenue}}{\text{Total Assets}}$
- $\text{Financial Leverage} = \frac{\text{Total Assets}}{\text{Total Equity}}$

## Hurdle Rates & Classification

| Condition | Classification | Status | Principle Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| $\text{ROE} \ge 15.0\% \land \text{Margin} \ge 15.0\% \land \text{Lev} \le 2.5\times$ | Pristine Compounding Quality | PASS | 100 |
| $\text{ROE} \ge 12.0\% \land \text{Lev} \le 3.5\times$ | Sound Operational ROE | PASS | 60 |
| $\text{Lev} > 3.5\times$ | Artificial Leverage Risk | WARN | 20 |
| $\text{ROE} \ge 8.0\%$ | Tepid ROE Compounding | WARN | 30 |
| $\text{ROE} < 8.0\%$ | Sub-par Capital Efficiency | FAIL | 0 |

Broad-market index ETFs (e.g. VOO, SPY, QQQ) automatically pass with a score of 100 due to natural basket diversification.

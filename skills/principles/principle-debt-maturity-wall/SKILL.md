# principle-debt-maturity-wall

Evaluates balance sheet stress across 3 solvency pillars:
1. **Net Debt to EBITDA Leverage**: Fortress net cash ($\le 0$) or $\le 1.0\times$ (max 40 pts).
2. **Debt Maturity Wall (24 Months)**: Checks if liquid cash and operating cash flow cover 100% of debt obligations maturing within 24 months (max 40 pts).
3. **Interest Coverage**: EBIT / Interest Expense $\ge 5.0\times$ (max 20 pts).

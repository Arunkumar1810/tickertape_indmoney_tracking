# Charlie Munger: Mental Models & Inversion Latticework

> *“I've always believed in the discipline of mastering the best that other people have figured out, rather than sitting there trying to dream it up yourself.”* — Charlie Munger

## 1. The Latticework of Mental Models
No single academic discipline or financial metric holds all the truth. A sound investor constructs a latticework of mental models across multiple disciplines:
- **Inversion (Algebra)**: Turn problems upside down. Figure out how to guarantee bankruptcy or failure, and avoid those paths.
- **Lollapalooza Effect (Psychology)**: Extreme outcomes occur when multiple psychological biases (social proof, incentive bias, commitment-consistency) point in the same direction.
- **Incentive-Caused Bias (Economics/Psychology)**: People rationalize whatever rewards their self-interest. Never trust corporate cheerleading where stock options reward reckless risk-taking.
- **Autocatalysis (Chemistry/Biology)**: Self-reinforcing competitive advantages that create compounding network effects.
- **Margin of Safety (Engineering)**: Build a bridge that supports 30,000 lbs even if only 10,000 lb trucks drive across it.

## 2. Rejection of Cigar Butts
Munger famously transformed Berkshire Hathaway from buying dying textile mills at statistical discounts into owning extraordinary businesses (See's Candies, Coca-Cola, Apple) at fair prices.
- A mediocre business at a low P/E will suffer eroding capital compounding.
- An elite compounding machine (ROIC $> 20\%$) with pricing power justifies a fair multiple.

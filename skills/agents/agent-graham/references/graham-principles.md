# Benjamin Graham: The Dean of Wall Street & Margin of Safety

> *“An investment operation is one which, upon thorough analysis, promises safety of principal and an adequate return. Operations not meeting these requirements are speculative.”* — Benjamin Graham, *Security Analysis* (1934)

## Core Graham Tenets

### 1. Mr. Market
The market is a manic-depressive business partner who comes by every day offering to buy your interest or sell you his at varying prices. You do not accept his opinion of value; you exploit his emotional distress.

### 2. The Margin of Safety
The secret of sound investment can be distilled into three words: **Margin of Safety**. The function of the margin of safety is to render an accurate estimate of future earnings unnecessary. If a stock is purchased below its liquid asset liquidation value, the investor has a cushion against adverse developments.

### 3. Net-Net Working Capital (NNWC)
Graham's most conservative metric:
- Cash and short-term equivalents at 100%
- Receivables at 75%
- Inventory at 50%
- Less 100% of all liabilities (both current and long-term)
If a stock sells below NNWC, you are essentially getting the operating business and all fixed assets for free.

### 4. The Graham Number
$$\text{Graham Number} = \sqrt{22.5 \times \text{EPS} \times \text{Book Value per Share}}$$
Based on the premise that a defensive investor should not pay more than $15\times$ earnings and $1.5\times$ book value ($15 \times 1.5 = 22.5$).
